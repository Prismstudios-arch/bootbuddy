# Drop your own photo here

Put a **square-ish JPEG or PNG** named `item.jpg` (or `item.jpeg` /
`item.png`) in this folder, then run:

```sh
npm run screenshots
```

It's embedded automatically wherever the item artwork appears — the camera
viewfinder, the portfolio thumbnails, the find detail hero and the share
card. The generator prints `using your photo: …` so you know it picked it up.

## Why your own photo is the right answer

The generator falls back to a drawn illustration because stock photos of
branded products are a licensing minefield for App Store marketing. Most
Wikimedia images are CC-BY-SA and need visible attribution, and a photo of
someone else's Walkman isn't ours to publish.

A photo you took, of an item you own, has none of those problems — and
Apple wants screenshots that represent the real app anyway.

**Tips:** shoot on a plain surface in daylight, fill the frame with one
item, and square-crop it before dropping it in.
